Begin mediation

Select either Bid or Ask and your value. Additionally you can modify the number of days for the offer to stand and for the result to stand. We have pre-set 5 days for each to give both parties some time :-)

Bid - is the price a buyer is willing to pay as a maximum.

Ask - is the price a seller wants to receive as a minimum.


So this is the process:
  • choose if you provide an ask or a bid
  • choose your value
    • the minimum you would accept when you have chosen Ask
    • the maximum you would pay when you have chosen Bid
  • optionally change the number of days
  • hit submit
