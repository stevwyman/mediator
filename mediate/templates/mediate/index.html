Hi and welcome ...

... to the free online mediation

Stuck in your negotiation? Don't know if a settlement can be reached? We are here to help you!

Both parties in a negotiation give us their reserve prices and we check for you if a potential negotiation has any chance of success.

Quick example:

  • Sarah would like to sell here old bike. Andrew is interested and ask her what she would to get for it.
  • Sarah expects at least 30 apples.
  • Andrew is willing to pay 20 apples.

As they are both very skilled negotiators, they both have a number in their heads of either an upper limit or a lower limit. Those numbers are their reserve prices. For Andrew is not willing to give x apples and Sarah will not give away here old bike for less then y apples.

They both negotiate, but cannot come to an agreement.

Now a little bird is flying by and asks them why they both looked so unhappy. The bird offers them to listen to each of them separately to learn their reserve prices.

There are now two scenarios:

  • And after that, the bird says: "You both are lucky - our reserve prices overlap and therefore you can reach an agreement."
  • Or, the bird says: "You both cannot reach an agreement - your reserve prices are too far away, sorry."

In both cases, Sarah and Andrew have some clarity. And in the positive case, they can restart their negotiation, knowing they have both to move a bit more. And in the case without a possible agreement, they know that they will not be able to to come to an agreement. And maybe they go and drink a lemonade together.

And this little tool here is the little bird :-)
